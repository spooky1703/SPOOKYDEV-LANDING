Laboratorio San Martín

An end-to-end clinical management system for the complete digitization of laboratories, from intake to results delivery.

The Challenge

Clinical laboratories face slow, error-prone manual processes in patient management, results capture and report delivery, compromising operational efficiency and the patient experience.

The Solution

A full-stack SaaS platform that digitized 100% of the laboratory's operation, from intake to results delivery with automatic validation through QR codes.
