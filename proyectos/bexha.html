BEXHA CONTROL

A Smart Irrigation Control System for optimizing water resources and maximizing agricultural yield.

The Challenge

The Section 14 Water Users Association faced critical problems controlling its registries, together with slow logistics for validating irrigation in the field, which caused operational inefficiencies and significant water losses.

The Solution

We built a hybrid ecosystem (Desktop + Mobile) that digitized 100% of their operation, eliminating paper and optimizing validation times with QR technology and real-time synchronization.

Solution Architecture

Admin Panel

Full control of landholders, printing of receipts with encrypted QR codes and automatic financial reports.

Offline Field App

Instant receipt validation without internet, a visual irrigation matrix and per-device security lock.

Centralized Database

Secure storage of every historical record with automatic synchronization across platforms.

Automated Reports

Instant generation of billing reports, registries and financial analysis, exportable to Excel and PDF.
