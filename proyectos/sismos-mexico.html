Machine Learning & Data Science

Seismic Patterns

Data analysis and machine learning focused on the seismic patterns of Guerrero, Michoacán and Oaxaca, analyzing more than 219,000 historical events.

The Challenge

Mexico is a highly seismic country. Understanding the classification and patterns of earthquakes based on variables such as latitude, longitude and depth is vital for geological studies and disaster prevention.

The Solution

We built Machine Learning models (both supervised and unsupervised) that cluster, classify and identify anomalies (historic earthquakes) in the National Seismological Service database.

Analysis Components

Data Architecture

Preprocessing

Transformation of the raw SSN format into a structured, workable dataset with 219,712 seismic events analyzed.

EDA

Exploratory analysis to understand distributions, magnitudes and correlations, such as the high concentration of earthquakes in Oaxaca.

Supervised Models

Earthquake classification using Logistic Regression, Decision Tree and Random Forest with high accuracy.

Unsupervised Models

Cluster identification with K-Means and detection of atypical earthquakes (major quakes) using Isolation Forest.
