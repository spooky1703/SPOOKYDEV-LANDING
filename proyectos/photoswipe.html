Photo Swipe Organizer

A native iOS app to organize your gallery with swipe gestures. Retro-tech terminal style interface with 100% local processing.

The Concept

Many people have thousands of photos piled up on their iPhone and find it tedious to review them one by one to delete the ones they do not want.

The Solution

An app that presents photos attractively and lets you decide quickly with gestures: swipe left to keep, right to delete.

Capabilities

Key Features

Smart Filters

Filter by screenshots, selfies, Live Photos, duplicates and more. Focus on what you actually need to review.

Swipe Interface

Swipe left (keep) or right (delete). Visual feedback with colors and haptic feedback with vibrations.

Terminal Style

A unique design inspired by retro-tech terminals. Customize your username and choose between English and Spanish.

100% Private

Fully local processing. No data ever leaves your iPhone. Zero analytics, zero tracking.

Batch Sessions

Review 10, 20, 50 or 100 photos per session. Perfect for cleaning up your gallery in spare moments.

Safe Deletion

Review every marked photo before deleting permanently. You will never lose something by mistake.
